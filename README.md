## 🕹️ About

Songs of Glimmerwick is a story-driven witch academy RPG set in a musical woodland fantasy world. Enrolled at Glimmerwick’s university of magic, you cast spells through music, attend classes, grow the university garden, and build relationships with classmates and townsfolk. Developed and published by Eastshade Studios, the game is scheduled to launch on September 30, 2026, and is currently in pre-release.

A peaceful, single-player adventure for players who enjoy cozy RPGs, magical school stories, exploration, farming, and relationship-focused storytelling.

## ✨ Features

- 🎵 **Musical Magic** — Use a flute and songbook to cast spells and explore the island.
- 🏫 **Witch Academy** — Attend classes and join after-school clubs at Glimmerwick’s magic university.
- 🌱 **University Garden** — Grow and tend the university garden with the help of ancient songs.
- 💬 **Living Relationships** — Make friends with classmates and townsfolk through character-driven stories.
- 🗺️ **Woodland Exploration** — Discover the island’s oddities, mysteries, and hidden corners.
- 🎉 **Changing Seasons** — Experience a year of seasons, festivals, curses, and witchy hijinks.
- 📖 **Story-Rich RPG** — Follow a main story alongside smaller tales about Glimmerwick’s inhabitants.
